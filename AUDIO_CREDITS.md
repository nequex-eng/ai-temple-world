# Temple audio credits

The browser soundscape uses the following recordings, loaded only after the visitor enables sound:

- **Courtyard birdsong** — “Birds chirping in a garden.ogg”, recorded by Akum20 on 16 May 2025. Public-domain dedication (PD-self). Original: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Birds_chirping_in_a_garden.ogg
- **Buddhist chant** — “Om mani padme hum in Dingjue Temple, Chunghua, Taiwan”, recorded by Saimmx on 2 June 2026. CC0 1.0. Original: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:20260602_145912_Dingjue_Om_mani_padme_hum.ogg
- **Meditative gong resonance** — “Gong55.ogg”, by Stephan, 28 August 2024. CC0 1.0. Original: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gong55.ogg

The courtyard birdsong is the continuous outdoor ambience after the visitor explicitly enables sound. The Buddhist chant fades in near the Mahavira Hall; the gong recording is used when the visitor enables sound and when the bell is rung near the bell pavilion. A quiet wind layer and soft stone footsteps are generated locally by the Web Audio API. MP3 transcodes are preferred for broad mobile-browser compatibility, with original Ogg files retained as fallbacks.
